Career Details

Environmental Scientist 🌱

Environmental Scientists study environmental problems and develop solutions related to pollution control, conservation, climate change and sustainability.

Average Salary in Malaysia

RM3,500 - RM9,000 per month

Required Skills

  • Scientific Research
  • Laboratory Analysis
  • Data Interpretation
  • Critical Thinking
  • Problem Solving

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree in Environmental Science / Chemistry / Biology
  • Master's Degree for advanced research positions
  • PhD for academic or specialist careers

Career Opportunities

Government agencies, environmental consulting companies, research institutions, universities and industrial sectors.

Future Demand

★★★★★
High demand due to sustainability development, climate change management and environmental protection.
